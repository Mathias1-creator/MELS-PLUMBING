About MELS PLUMBING.

Veteran owned and operated, working out of North San Diego County for more than twenty years.

The owner

Melvin P. Nelson

MELS PLUMBING is Melvin P. Nelson. He served, came home, and went straight into the trade — more than twenty years of it now, most of that spent under sinks and behind walls in Escondido, Vista, Murrieta and Temecula. His name is on the truck, which means the work is his to answer for.

The military teaches you to show up when you said you would, to check your own work before someone else has to, and to give a straight report even when it is not the one anybody wants to hear. That is how this business runs. If a repair will hold for another five years, you will hear that. If your water heater is on borrowed time, you will hear that too, with the price attached, before you have committed to anything.

Every house gets treated the way he would want his own treated: drop cloths down, the job site swept before the truck leaves, and no surprises added to the invoice at the end. Most of the work now comes from people who got told the truth on a small job and called back for a bigger one. That is the whole business plan, and after twenty years it has not needed changing.

How we work

Three things you can count on.

Honesty first

You get the real diagnosis and the real price before any work starts. When the smaller fix will hold, that is the one we recommend, even though the bigger job pays better.

On-time every time

When we give you a window, we keep it. If the job before yours runs long, you get a phone call with a new time — not silence and a wasted afternoon.

Veteran discipline

Clean work, checked twice, and a job site left the way we found it. Habits picked up in the service that turned out to make for good plumbing.

Talk to Melvin directly.

No call center, no dispatcher reading a script. Call or text and you are talking to the plumber who will be doing the work.
