Services

Our services.

Remodels, repairs and maintenance for homes and rentals in Escondido, Vista, Murrieta and Temecula.

01 — Remodels

Plumbing remodels

A remodel goes sideways when the plumbing is treated as an afterthought. We come in early, look at where your drains and supply lines actually run, and tell you what your new layout will take before the tile goes down.

Kitchen or bathroom, a single fixture move or a whole-house re-pipe, you get the same thing: work that passes inspection and a schedule we hold to, so the other trades are not left standing around.

  • Kitchen and bathroom rough-in
  • Fixture and faucet installation
  • Whole or partial re-piping
  • Drain and vent line relocation
  • Shower valve and tub sets
  • New construction plumbing

02 — Repairs

Plumbing repairs

Most repair calls start with a guess about what is wrong. We find the actual source first — a running toilet and a slab leak can look the same on your water bill — then tell you what it costs to fix.

You approve the price before we start. If the honest answer is that a fixture is worth replacing instead of repairing, we will tell you that and let you make the call.

  • Leak detection and pipe repair
  • Clogged drains and stoppages
  • Water heater repair and replacement
  • Garbage disposal repair
  • Toilet repair and replacement
  • Faucet and valve replacement
Call about a repair
03 — Maintenance

Plumbing maintenance

The cheapest plumbing problem is the one caught early. A yearly look at your supply lines, angle stops, water heater and drains is what keeps a flooded hallway from ever happening.

Landlords and property managers: we can run the same checklist across every unit and hand you one clear report on what needs attention now and what can wait until next year.

  • Preventative plumbing inspections
  • Drain cleaning
  • Water heater flush and inspection
  • Angle stop and supply line checks
  • Seasonal checkups for rentals
  • Pre-purchase plumbing walkthrough
